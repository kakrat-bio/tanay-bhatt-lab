ABOUT

A virtual group.
A collaborative approach.

Bhatt Research Lab connects researchers across institutions through shared questions in skin biology, infection and biological measurement.

How we work

Our work spans skin biology, infection, virology and biological reference standards, bringing biological questions together with the methods needed to study them reliably.

Research takes place at different collaborating institutions. The group includes project associates, collaborating researchers and dissertation students, with members contributing experimental and computational perspectives.

The site also describes academic collaborations and R&D involving Koshkey. These projects are presented through their scientific questions and research scope.

AFFILIATIONS & DISCLOSURES

People, projects
and institutions

  • Reference Standard Project

    Tanay Bhatt is Principal Investigator of the Reference Standard Project, a collaboration between the National Centre for Biological Sciences (NCBS) and the Centre for Cellular and Molecular Platforms (C-CAMP).

  • Colin Jamora Laboratory

    Tanay Bhatt collaborates with the Colin Jamora Laboratory. This collaboration forms part of the group’s wider work in skin biology and tissue repair.

  • Koshkey

    Tanay Bhatt is a co-founder of Koshkey. This website includes academic collaborations and R&D involving Koshkey, described in scientific terms.

Bhatt Research Lab is a virtual research group. Members may work at different host institutions; a person’s participation in the group does not imply an appointment at every collaborating organisation.

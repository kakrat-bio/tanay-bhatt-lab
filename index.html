EPITHELIAL BIOLOGY · TISSUE REPAIR · INNATE IMMUNITY

Understanding how injured epithelia repair themselves—and defend against infection.

The Bhatt Lab investigates the cellular mechanisms that connect tissue injury, wound repair and antimicrobial defense, with a focus on epithelial cells and the innate immune molecules they deploy.
